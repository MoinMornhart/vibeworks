Your vibe coding projects.
In one place. On your server.

Ideas, tasks, notes, commits, CI and Claude Code – VibeWorks gathers everything in one app that runs on your Proxmox and updates itself.

Install on Proxmox ⬇️ Windows app Read the docs

Installed in a minute

Run it on the Proxmox VE host – the installer creates a Debian container, installs everything and sets up automatic updates.

bash -c "$(curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MoinMornhart/vibeworks/main/install/proxmox.sh)"

Works without Proxmox too – a Debian or Ubuntu server is enough. Installation guide →

What's inside

🗂️

Projects

Grid, list or kanban, status from idea to done. Progress can determine itself – from tasks, commits, CI and planning.

🔀

Tasks ↔ issues

Tasks become issues on GitHub, GitLab or Gitea and sync back. Commits, CI status and a dependency check come with it.

🤖

Claude Code

VibeWorks is an MCP server: Claude reads and completes your tasks, writes notes and docs – plus a CLAUDE.md generator and a prompt library.

🪟

Windows app

Its own window, tray, quick capture with Ctrl+Alt+V from any program and Windows notifications. Updates itself.

☀️

Today & focus

Plan tasks from all projects for the day, track time, 25-minute focus timer. The weekly review shows where the time went.

🔥

Review

An activity grid over the whole year, streaks, small achievements and a timeline across all projects.

🌐

Live monitoring

Checks the finished site every 5 minutes: availability, response time, SSL expiry – with a notification when something goes down.

💶

Costs

Hosting, domain, AI APIs per project – totals per month and year, a warning two weeks before every renewal.

📥

Idea inbox

Drop ideas via “share” on your phone, a drop address or ntfy – sort them into projects or tasks later.

🔔

Notifications

Due tasks, red CI, outages, access requests – via ntfy to your phone, webhook (Discord, Slack) or email.

🪦

Project graveyard

Whatever has slept for 30 days asks: carry on or bury – with a tombstone, last words and resurrection.

🖼️

Portfolio & sharing

Share projects via link, invite people – and show selected projects as a public portfolio.

🎨

Your design

Animated backgrounds, your own colors, light or dark, glass effect – per account. English and German.

🔐

Secure

Passkeys, two-factor, CSP, CSRF and SSRF protection, encrypted tokens. Your data stays on your server.

🚀

Low maintenance

One command installs everything, updates arrive every 15 minutes – with backup, health check and automatic rollback.

What it looks like

Working with Claude Code

  1. Create a task – in VibeWorks, via quick capture or from the idea inbox.
  2. Connect Claude – one API key, one command: claude mcp add …
  3. “Work through the open tasks” – Claude fetches the tasks, sets them to “In progress”, implements them and checks them off.
  4. Everything stays visible – history, issues, commits and CI follow along.
